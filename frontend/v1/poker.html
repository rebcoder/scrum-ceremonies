How to Use Planning Poker

Select Your Card

Choose a card value that represents your estimate for the backlog item. Use Fibonacci numbers (1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 20) or the coffee cup (☕) if you need clarification.

Team Collaboration

All team members vote independently. Wait for everyone to submit their votes before revealing results to avoid bias.

Reveal Votes

Once everyone has voted, reveal all votes to see the team's estimates. Discuss any significant differences in estimates.

Discuss & Re-vote

If estimates vary widely, discuss the story requirements and vote again until the team reaches consensus.

Track Progress

Use the room code to return to the same room later. All participants and votes are synchronized in real time.

Best Practices

Keep stories small and well-defined. Use relative sizing rather than absolute time estimates. Focus on complexity, not effort.

Planning Poker Best Practices

  • Time-box discussions: Keep story discussions focused and under 5 minutes
  • Use Fibonacci sequence: The gaps between numbers help teams avoid false precision
  • Vote independently: Don't discuss estimates before everyone has voted
  • Clarify requirements: Use the coffee cup (☕) if the story needs more detail
  • Reach consensus: Discuss differences and re-vote until the team agrees

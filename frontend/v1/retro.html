How to Use Sprint Retrospectives

Went Well

Celebrate successes! Add cards for things that worked well during the sprint. These are practices worth continuing.

Improve

Identify challenges and blockers. Add cards for areas that need attention or caused friction during the sprint.

Improvement Actions

Turn insights into actions! Create specific, measurable tasks the team will commit to for the next sprint.

Team Voting

Vote on cards to prioritize discussion topics. Focus on the most important issues identified by the team.

Search & Filter

Use the search bar to find specific topics. Sort by votes to see the highest priority items first.

Export Results

Export your retrospective as a PDF to share with stakeholders and track progress over time.

Best Practices for Effective Retrospectives

  • Time-box discussions: Keep each topic focused and under 5 minutes
  • Be specific: Use concrete examples rather than vague statements
  • Focus on improvement: Frame problems as opportunities to learn
  • Make it safe: Create an environment where everyone can speak openly
  • Follow through: Track action items and review progress in the next retro

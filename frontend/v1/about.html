About Scrum Ceremonies

What is Scrum Ceremonies?

Scrum Ceremonies is a simple web-based tool that helps Agile teams run their Scrum ceremonies more effectively. We provide lightweight tools for Planning Poker (story estimation), Sprint Retrospectives, and Team Mood Check - the essential ceremonies that help teams plan better, improve continuously, and monitor team health.

No accounts, no sign-ups, no complexity. Just create a room, share a link, and start collaborating with your team.

Why I Built Scrum Ceremonies

I built Scrum Ceremonies because I was tired of complex tools getting in the way of simple collaboration. Many Agile tools require teams to sign up, configure workspaces, and navigate through layers of features they don't need.

Scrum Ceremonies focuses on doing three things well: helping teams estimate stories through Planning Poker, facilitating productive Sprint Retrospectives, and tracking team health with Team Mood Check. That's it. No feature bloat, no learning curve.

Our Tools

Planning Poker

Estimate user stories together with your team. Everyone votes simultaneously to avoid bias, then discuss differences to reach consensus. Perfect for sprint planning and backlog refinement sessions.

Sprint Retrospectives

Run effective retrospectives with a simple three-column board: what went well, what to improve, and action items. Team members can add cards, vote on priorities, and collaborate in real-time - no matter where they are.

Team Mood Check

Monitor and track your team's emotional well-being and satisfaction. Team members can quickly share their mood anonymously, helping identify when the team needs support or celebration. Perfect for regular pulse checks and understanding team dynamics.

What Makes Scrum Ceremonies Different

  • No Registration Required: Jump in and start using it immediately. No email, no password, no accounts to manage.
  • Simple and Fast: Create a room in seconds, share the code, and your team is collaborating.
  • Real-Time Collaboration: See updates instantly as your team members participate - perfect for remote teams.
  • Privacy-Focused: Sessions are temporary. When you're done, the data expires. No permanent storage of your team's work.
  • Works on Any Device: Use it on your laptop, tablet, or phone - anywhere you have a web browser.
  • Free to Use: Always free. No freemium limits, no paywalls, no surprises.

How It Works

Using Scrum Ceremonies couldn't be simpler:

  1. One person creates a room and gets a room code
  2. Share the room code or link with your team
  3. Team members join, pick a display name, and start collaborating
  4. When your session is done, the room data automatically expires

That's it. No setup, no configuration, no hassle.

Who Uses Scrum Ceremonies?

Scrum Ceremonies is used by:

  • Agile software development teams running Scrum ceremonies
  • Scrum Masters facilitating planning and retrospectives
  • Product Owners leading sprint planning sessions
  • Remote and distributed teams who need simple collaboration tools
  • Startups and small teams looking for tools without the overhead
  • Anyone practicing Agile who wants to focus on collaboration, not tool configuration

How Scrum Ceremonies Stays Free

It is free because it is open source, MIT-licensed, and there is no service to pay for. No ads, no analytics, no accounts, no telemetry of any kind. You run it yourself — the whole stack is a Spring Boot service, some static files, and a Redis instance.

If you find it useful, the best support is a star on the repository, a bug report, or a pull request.

Being Honest About Limitations

Scrum Ceremonies is a small open-source project. Being upfront about what that means:

  • This is an early-stage product, and things may change as it evolves
  • There is no hosted service and no uptime guarantee — you run your own instance
  • Features may be added or removed based on what makes sense for the product
  • Your feedback helps shape the direction of Scrum Ceremonies

Issues and pull requests are welcome.

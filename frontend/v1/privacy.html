Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Note on legal review. This policy is written in plain English to describe, accurately, what the Scrum Ceremonies free tools actually do with your data (grounded in how the software is built). It has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. Before relying on it for formal compliance (GDPR, CCPA, etc.), a qualified attorney should review it. If anything here is unclear, open an issue in the project's GitHub repository.

Introduction

This policy covers the Scrum Ceremonies free tools — Planning Poker, Sprint Retrospective, and Team Mood Check. You join with a link and a room code — no registration, no account — and the data involved is temporary and session-based.

Information We Collect

  • Display names: the name you choose when joining a room or board — temporary, used only during your active session.
  • Session content: cards, votes, comments, estimates, and mood responses you create during a session — stored temporarily while the room is active.

Automatically collected

  • Server logs: standard logs including IP addresses, browser type, and access times, used for security, rate limiting, and troubleshooting.
  • Technical information: device type, screen size, and browser version for compatibility and optimization.

How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the free tools
  • Enable real-time collaboration in your session
  • Understand usage and improve the tools
  • Prevent abuse (e.g. per-IP rate limits) and enforce our terms

We do not sell your personal information.

Data Storage & Retention

  • Sessions: room codes, votes, cards, and mood responses are transient and are automatically deleted after a period of inactivity (rooms expire on the order of hours).
  • Server logs: retained for a limited time (typically 30-90 days) for security and troubleshooting, then deleted.

There is no persistent user database in this product: nothing you enter survives beyond the room's lifetime.

Cookies & Local Storage

  • Security cookies: a CSRF-protection cookie and a session cookie used to protect requests and apply per-session limits.
  • Local storage: display preferences (such as light/dark theme) so your settings persist on your device.

You can clear cookies and local storage in your browser at any time.

Third-Party Services

Advertising

There is none. These pages serve no advertisements, load no ad or analytics scripts, and set no advertising cookies.

Service providers

We use cloud hosting to run the tools. Hosting providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies.

Your Rights & Choices

  • Deletion: session data deletes itself when rooms expire; closing your room ends it sooner.
  • Cookies & ads: manage cookies in your browser and opt out of ad personalization via the tools above.
  • Contact: for a self-hosted instance, contact whoever operates it — they hold the data. For questions about the software itself, open an issue in the project's GitHub repository.

Depending on where you live (e.g. the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA), you may have additional rights. We honor valid requests (see the legal-review note at the top).

Security

We implement reasonable safeguards — transport encryption, per-IP rate limiting, and room-scoped access by code — to protect session data. No internet service is perfectly secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Scrum Ceremonies is a workplace tool and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the product evolves or as legal requirements change. We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top. Continued use after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

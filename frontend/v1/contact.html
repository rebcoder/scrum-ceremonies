Response Time

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24-48 hours during business days. For urgent technical issues affecting your team's ability to use Scrum Ceremonies, we will prioritize your request.

Your feedback helps us improve Scrum Ceremonies. We welcome suggestions for new features, improvements to existing tools, or ways to make the service more useful for Agile teams worldwide.
